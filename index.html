Privacy policy

Effective date: 14, Sep, 2026
App: AI Companion
Developer / data controller: Viral Shah
Privacy contact: viralshahsupport@gmail.com

This policy explains how Viral Shah collects, uses, stores and discloses information when you use AI Companion, contact us, or visit this website. AI Companion provides conversations with fictional adult AI characters. It is intended for people aged 18 and older.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: your account identifier, email address and information supplied by your sign-in provider, which may include a display name and profile image. Authentication providers process credentials and session information to sign you in.
  • Conversation information: messages you send, AI replies, timestamps and the selected companion. Recent conversation context is sent to our AI provider to generate responses.
  • Preferences and usage: the companion and language you select, app preferences where saved, and account-linked usage counters used to apply limits and protect the service.
  • Support information: your email address, request details and correspondence when you contact us or request deletion.
  • Technical information: our hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request times, device/browser details, authentication events and operational logs to deliver and secure their services.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate your account, generate AI responses, maintain recent conversations, apply your preferences and usage limits, respond to requests, investigate misuse and operate the app. We also use information where necessary to meet applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing it, legal obligations, or consent for activities that require consent. You can contact us about your rights or withdraw consent where it applies.

3. Service providers and disclosures

  • Google Firebase: authentication, server functions and Firestore storage process account information, conversation records and service requests. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Gemini API: receives conversation messages, recent context, companion instructions and the chosen language to generate replies. See the Gemini API terms and Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and may process technical access information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Support email provider: Gmail processes messages sent to our support address.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, or defend legal rights. We do not publish your conversations as public profiles or public posts.

4. Storage, security and international processing

App requests are transmitted over HTTPS. Our infrastructure providers supply access controls and managed storage protections. This is not end-to-end encrypted chat: our backend and AI provider must process message content to provide the service. No security measure is infallible.

5. Retention

  • Account and app records: kept while your account is active unless you delete them earlier. The current chat service retains a rolling history of up to 40 messages for each companion; older messages are removed from that active history as new exchanges are saved.
  • Verified account-deletion requests: completed within 0 days after necessary ownership verification, unless a lawful exception applies and is explained to you.
  • Backups: deleted data expires from backups within 0 days. If a backup is restored, applicable deletion requests are reapplied before the restored data returns to normal use.
  • Operational and security logs: retained for 0 days.
  • Support correspondence: retained for 1 days after the request is resolved.

Removing records from our app database does not establish that every independent provider log is immediately removed. Provider retention and any applicable deletion arrangements are described above.

6. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing viralshahsupport@gmail.com. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of information, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. We may verify ownership before responding to protect your account.

To delete your account and associated information, follow the account-deletion instructions below. Uninstalling the app does not delete a server account.

7. Adults only

The app is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly provide accounts to children. If you believe someone under 18 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. This website

This page contains no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or forms that submit information to us. Email links open your email application; a request is sent only when you send the email. GitHub may process technical information to host and protect this website.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or our practices change. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice where required. Contact Viral Shah at viralshahsupport@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Delete your AI Companion account

Developer: Viral Shah

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by email, even if you have uninstalled the app. You do not need to reinstall it or create another account to make a request.

Request account deletion by email

If the button does not open your email app, send an email manually to viralshahsupport@gmail.com with the subject AI Companion account deletion request.

Clicking the button alone does not submit a request or delete your account. Review the email and send it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from your account email address when possible. Include the email used for the app and your sign-in method, such as Google or email.
  2. If you cannot access that email, explain this in your request. We will arrange a reasonable alternative ownership check.
  3. We may ask you to verify ownership before deleting information. Do not send passwords, verification codes, authentication tokens, payment card details or copies of private conversations.
  4. After verification, we complete the request within 0 days and email you when it is completed, or explain any lawful exception.

What account deletion removes

Account deletion removes the AI Companion sign-in account and the personal app data associated with it, including stored conversation history for all six companions, saved profile/preferences and saved memories where present, and account-linked app usage records, except the specifically disclosed retention exceptions below. Account deletion is permanent.

Any data retained and why: 0

Backups expire within 0 days. Support correspondence is retained for 1 days after resolution and operational/security logs for 0 days. See the privacy policy for provider processing and retention details.

Delete conversations without deleting your account

You may instead email the same address with the subject AI Companion conversation deletion request. State whether you want all companion conversations deleted or name the companion. This request keeps your sign-in account active.

Subscriptions and app removal

If you have an active store subscription, manage or cancel it through Google Play subscriptions or your device's App Store subscription settings. An account-deletion request does not by itself cancel a store subscription. You may submit your deletion request while arranging cancellation; contact us if you need help.

Deleting this app account does not delete your Google or Apple account. Signing out, uninstalling the app or clearing one conversation is not the same as deleting your AI Companion account.
